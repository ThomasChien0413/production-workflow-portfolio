# Portfolio collaboration and safety rules

Read `AGENTS.md`, `DESIGN.md` and `PLAN.md` before changing this independent demo.

- This repository is a sanitized interview portfolio, not the company production repository or a deployment target.
- The owner has permission to share the code, all sheet layouts and letterheads. Do not copy customer orders, employee records, company operational handoffs, credentials or live infrastructure identifiers.
- Preserve authorized template fields, versioning, role enforcement, workflow rules and responsive blue-and-white UI. Demo branding is distinct from the live application.
- Use fresh Git history. Never copy the original `.git`, `.env`, caches, database dumps, browser authentication state, deployment artifacts or private issue/PR history.
- Claim work in PLAN before implementation and record exact verification/limits. Preserve unrelated changes.
- Public CI uses standard GitHub-hosted Ubuntu/Windows runners and read-only permissions; never attach a company or personal self-hosted runner. Do not bypass audit, CSRF, lockout, authorization or failing-test gates.
- Seed only an explicitly local/demo database. All demo accounts/values are synthetic. Never connect this copy to the company database or copy its web-push key pair.
- No public visibility, production deployment, secret rotation or paid hosting without explicit user authorization. Prepare privately for review first.
- Do not invent contribution claims or automatically assign a redistribution license to company code. Preserve third-party font/library notices.

Traditional Chinese UI and Asia/Taipei business dates remain intentional product features. Documentation may be English.
